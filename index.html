Jaguar Land Rover

Carbon footprint information system

2026 · Team of 6

Redesigned how carbon data flows from suppliers to reporting, built around the SAP S/4HANA and Anaplan stack already in place.

Estimated £290–580m of value from a 1–2% efficiency gain.

How it worked

Analysed the carbon data process across five lifecycle stages and found supplier visibility stopped at Tier 1, relying on annual self-declarations and delayed plant invoices. Co-designed a replacement using IoT sensors, Kafka data streaming, per-vehicle digital twins and blockchain, and assessed EU Battery Regulation requirements and risks across supplier data governance, sensor integrity and regional emission factors.
